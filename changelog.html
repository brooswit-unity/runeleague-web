v0.0.39 — v0.0.39 — Whoosh by speed, changelog page

2026-10-03 18:12:42 UTC

Whoosh and draft particles follow speed, and a changelog page.

  • The draft whoosh sound and the white draft side trails now use one shared level: how fast you are compared with your current top speed, damped to zero as your draft fills. With little draft and high speed they are at their strongest; at full draft (20) they are silent and invisible. Before, they got stronger as draft rose, so this is the opposite direction.
  • New changelog page at /changelog.html, built from these release notes at release time (newest first), with a small Changelog link in a bar under the game. The bar makes the game window about 30 pixels shorter.
  • Build cache test again (no game change): the scan that checks the Unity cache for private data now ignores harmless matches, so this build may save the cache for the first time.
  • Proven in the editor tests: the new level table (speed 120 with no draft = 1, full draft = 0), no extra allocation, same trail cost as before, the changelog generator handles hostile text and renders all 36 earlier releases.
  • Unproven: how the whoosh and trails feel in a live race, the live fps, the changelog page and link on phones, and whether the cache is accepted (the next build is the first that could be faster).

v0.0.37 — v0.0.37 — Percent drafting, max 20

2026-10-03 15:27:13 UTC

Drafting is a percent boost now, and weaker.

  • Drafting used to add a flat amount to acceleration and top speed. Now a draft of d makes you d percent faster in both (draft 5 = 5% more acceleration and 5% more top speed). The draft maximum is 20, so the most you can get is +20%. Reverse speed is unchanged.
  • This is much weaker than before: at the old maximum you got +112% acceleration (17 instead of 8) and +75% top speed (210 instead of 120 m/s); now the maximum is 9.6 acceleration and 144 m/s. That is what Brooswit asked for ("Drafts were too strong. As written.").
  • The draft bar on screen shows how full your draft is (20 = 100%), it is not the speed bonus.
  • Bots follow the same new rule. In the editor test the first bot to finish a lap takes 63.8 s instead of 60.0 s, the median 79.7 s instead of 76.5 s, the slowest 94.6 s instead of 97.8 s, and wall touches drop from 708 to 379 per lap. The first-lap limit in the test was raised to match, which is a re-baseline and not a loosening.
  • Unproven: how it feels in a live race, whether racing is still fun with bots this slow, framerate with 64 cars on the live site.

v0.0.35 — v0.0.35 — Start diagnostics

Start diagnostics (no gameplay change).

  • Adds a small observer that prints a START_DIAG line to the browser console twice a second for the first 20 seconds of a race: speed, whether W is held, whether the car is grounded, whether the camera follows it and whether the page has focus. It only reads; it changes nothing in the game.

v0.0.34 — v0.0.34 — Rear thrusters, black wheel trails

2026-10-03 12:57:31 UTC

Rear thrusters and black wheel trails.

  • Every car now has two glowing exhaust stubs at the back and two coloured trails behind them. They brighten when the car is on the throttle and fade when it coasts.
  • The six wheel trails are now plain black. In the test pictures black shows on both the road and the floor.
  • For your own car and the bots the glow follows the real throttle. For other players online it is guessed from how fast their car is speeding up, so it under-reads when a car is at its top speed.
  • Proven in the editor tests: one shared draw for all trails, no memory growth over 600 frames; effects cost 6.2 ms per frame for 64 cars (was 4.8 ms). Bot laps in the isolated test still finish 60.0 to 97.8 s with nothing stuck.
  • Unproven: how it looks and runs in a live browser race, and the framerate with 64 cars on the live site. A test that ran the trail checks and the bot lap test in one go failed on this change and on the code before it, so I only trust the lap test when it runs on its own.
  • Build note: the build cache still does not hit (it only restores within the same release tag), so the build time is unchanged.

v0.0.33 — v0.0.33 — Bots pass slower cars

2026-10-03 02:05:29 UTC

Bots now pass slower cars.

  • A bot that catches a slower car on a straight steers out of that car's draft line (up to 4.5 m sideways) and passes, instead of sitting behind it. Bots still follow faster cars to draft as before. It does not do this at the start, in slow corners or on the final bend.
  • Proven in the editor tests: all 64 bots still finish laps with nothing stuck or off the road; best/median/worst lap 63.1/75.3/95.9 s, 683 wall touches (was 708). Bot thinking costs more: about 1.2 ms for 64 bots per physics step (was 0.17 ms).
  • Unproven: how the passing looks in a live race, the framerate on the live site with 64 bots, and how it behaves with other players online.
  • Build note: this should be the first release that restores the build cache, so the build time is the number to watch.

v0.0.32 — v0.0.32 — Stronger drafting

2026-10-03 01:34:57 UTC

Stronger drafting.

  • Drafting reaches further and builds faster: the trail you follow lasts 2 seconds (was 1), draft builds 3 times quicker, and a car picks up draft from 4 m away (was 2 m). A car that is itself in a lot of draft passes less on to the car behind it.
  • Bots brake earlier for the last bend of the lap so they stay on the road with the stronger draft. This costs a few more wall touches than before (about 708 instead of 565 per lap in the bot test); Brooswit approved that.
  • Proven in the editor tests: bot laps still finish with nothing stuck or off the road (best/median/worst 60.0/76.5/97.8 s), draft maths cost 0.46 / 1.07 ms for 32 / 64 cars.
  • Unproven: how it feels in a live race, framerate on the live site with 32 players, and whether the draft is now too strong (a straight line of cars can all reach full draft).
  • Build note: this is the first release with the build cache switched on, so it will not be faster yet.

v0.0.23 — v0.0.23 — Slipstream draft redesign

2026-10-02 20:33:02 UTC

RUNE-25: slipstream redesigned as Brooswit specified. Each car keeps a draft value (max 9) that decays over time and builds from other cars' draft nodes (a node about every 2 m along each car's path, expiring after 1 s, weight 1 at the newest to 0 at the oldest, 1 draft per second per node). Acceleration = 8 + draft; top speed target = 120 + 10 x draft. Replaces the +3/+20-per-path model from v0.0.21/v0.0.22. Steady draft behind 1 / 3 / 5 cars (synthetic test): 3.0 / 6.7 / 9.0. Draft cost measured 0.25 ms (32 cars) and 0.60 ms (64) per physics step in the editor, versus 0.20 / 0.76 for the old model. Nodes are placed exactly every 2 m with interpolated times (a deliberate detail so the trail does not depend on tick rate). Unproven: how it feels live; the higher top speed may not be visible on the track (cars reached about 65 m/s on the old track against a target of 120); behaviour with several real players over the network (every peer computes it from observed positions, only the owner applies force). Replaces v0.0.22 on the live site.
